Your phone, on autopilot.

TextFox is an Android app that sends text messages for you: on a schedule, as a reply, or when you miss a call.

Get it on Google PlaySee how it works

TRIGGER 1 / 4 · SCHEDULE

At the minute you choose.

Pick a time, the weekdays and how often. Every other week, one random day, or once on a date.

08:00 · Mon–Fri → Mom

TRIGGER 2 / 4 · ONE TAP

One tap, everyone knows.

Each macro can live as a home-screen widget. Tap it and a ready-made text goes to the whole list.

widget → Anna, Jonas

TRIGGER 3 / 4 · AUTO-REPLY

Answers while you're busy.

Reply to texts from a chosen sender or with a keyword. Quiet hours hold replies until you're back.

has "meeting" → reply

TRIGGER 4 / 4 · MISSED CALL

Missed it? They still hear from you.

When a call rings out or you decline it, the caller gets a text a few seconds later.

missed call → text back

// macros

Set it up once.

A macro is a trigger, one or more recipients and a message. Group them in folders, flip the master switch, and watch the next run time on every card.

  • Message variables like {weekday} and {recipient}
  • Folders with a master switch
  • Delivery receipts and a full log
