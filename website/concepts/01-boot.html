// macros.run()

Four ways to fire a macro.

macros.tfsettings.tfutf-8
01macro "Good morning, Mom" {
02 when schedule(08:00, mon–fri)
03 send "Good morning ☀️ Have a lovely {weekday}."
04 to Mom // ai: fresh wording every day
05}
06macro "Running late" {
07 when tap(home-screen widget)
08 send "Running 10 min late, sorry!"
09 to Anna, Jonas
10}
11macro "Out of office" {
12 when text(from: anyone, has: "meeting")
13 reply "In a meeting. Back at 14:00."
14}
15macro "Missed call text-back" {
16 when missedCall() // declined calls too
17 reply "Sorry I missed your call. I'll ring you back soon."
18}
