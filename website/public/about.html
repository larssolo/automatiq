Made by Lars Sohl.

TextFox is made by Lars Sohl, a graphic designer, photographer and creative coder from Denmark, and the founder of kodekid.app.

Design, photography and code

Lars works where design meets code: brand identities, photography, apps and websites that are built as carefully as they look. He is based in Denmark and works across most of Europe.

Day to day he works at Brandsurface, where he offers his experience with creative coding and digital solutions.

Why TextFox

Many of the texts we send are the same every week: good morning, running late, back on Monday, sorry I missed your call. TextFox puts those on autopilot, on your own phone, without an account, ads or tracking.

Questions or ideas? Write to hello@larssohl.dk.
