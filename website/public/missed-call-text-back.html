Missed-call text-back for Android.

When you can't pick up, TextFox texts the caller for you, a few seconds after the call. They know you saw it, and you can call back when it suits you.

Get it onGoogle Play

Early access on Google Play · Free · Android 8.0+

How it works.

  1. A call rings out, or you decline it

    TextFox notices the missed call on your phone. Declined calls count too, so one tap on the red button is enough.

  2. The caller gets your text

    Your message goes to the number that called, a few seconds later. Limit it to chosen callers if you like.

  3. You see it in the log

    Every text-back is logged with its time, and marked Delivered ✓✓ when your carrier confirms it.

Questions about missed-call text-back

Does it work when I decline a call?

Yes. Both calls that ring out and calls you decline trigger the text-back.

Can I limit it to certain callers?

Yes. An optional caller filter limits the text-back to the numbers you choose.

Which permissions does it need?

Phone state and call log access, to notice a missed call, and SMS, to send the text. Both are used only on your phone and are never uploaded.

How fast is the text sent?

Usually a few seconds after the call ends. Every send is logged and marked Delivered when your carrier confirms it.
