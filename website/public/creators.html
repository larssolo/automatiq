// how it works

Four steps, no contract.

TextFox is free for 30 days, then a one-time purchase on Google Play. Creators get it unlocked for good with a personal Google Play promo code.

  1. Tell us what you're making

    Send a short mail with your channel, your audience and your idea. A rough plan is plenty.

  2. Get your promo code

    We reply with a personal code. Redeem it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code, and TextFox is unlocked on your Google account for good.

  3. Make it your way

    Use the films, logos and screenshots below as you like in your video, post or article.

  4. Send us the link

    We'd love to see it, and we may share it on textfox.app.

Text to copy

TextFox is an Android app that sends your text messages for you: on a schedule, as an auto-reply, after a missed call or with one tap on a widget. Set it up once, and it just runs.

TextFox puts your everyday texts on autopilot. Send good morning at 08:00 on weekdays, reply automatically when you're busy, text people back when you miss their call, or send "running late" with one tap from your home screen. Optional AI writes a fresh version of each message with your own free Google Gemini key. Everything runs on your phone, with no account, no ads and no tracking. Free for 30 days, then a one-time purchase. www.textfox.app
