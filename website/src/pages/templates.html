// how it works

Three taps, no typing.

TextFox can import a macro from a plain JSON file. These are the ones people reach for most, so you don't start from a blank editor.

  1. Download

    Tap Download .json on a template below. It's a small text file, so open it in any editor if you're curious.

  2. Import

    In TextFox, go to Settings → Backup → Import and pick the file. The macro appears in your list, switched off.

  3. Make it yours

    Add recipients, change the words and the time, then switch it on. Nothing sends until you do.

// good to know

Before you switch one on.

  • They arrive switched off and with no recipients. Nothing is sent until you add who it should text and turn it on.
  • The curly-brace words are filled in when the text goes out. {modtager} becomes the recipient's contact name, {ugedag} the weekday, {dato} the date and {tid} the time. (Yes, the words are Danish. TextFox is made in Denmark.)
  • The AI template needs a free Gemini key. It takes a minute to get one, and the AI section shows how. Without a key it simply sends the plain text.
  • Importing a file again resets that macro to the template, so edit your own copy once and leave the file alone.
  • Android only. TextFox needs Android 8.0 or newer and sends ordinary SMS, so carrier rates apply.
