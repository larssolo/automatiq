Send a text with one tap.

Put any TextFox macro on your home screen as a widget. One tap sends a ready-made text to everyone on the list, without opening an app or typing a word.

{{PLAY}}

{{CTA_NOTE}}

How it works.

  1. Write the macro once

    A name, the message and one or more recipients. Each copy can include that person's name.

  2. Add the widget to your home screen

    One widget per macro. Place as many as you like, next to your other apps.

  3. Tap to send

    The text goes out straight away. The widget shows the last send, like "Last: 14:32 ✓", and stays in sync with scheduled and automatic sends.

Inside the app, every macro card also has a one-tap send button, and a long press gives you Send now, Duplicate, Enable and Delete.
